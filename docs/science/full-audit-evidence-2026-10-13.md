# Workout library evidence audit — 13 October 2026

## Finding

The library offers credible, goal-specific starting structures. The strongest support is for progressive resistance training, target-lift specificity, sufficient effort and recovery, and adjusting weekly work to the person. The research does **not** establish that every shipped combination is optimal or clinically validated. More routines, sets, complexity or mandatory failure would not establish better quality.

This audit inspected the current catalog, generated per-plan review, goal evidence, progression/selection code, source versions, regional workload accounting and training add-ons. Fresh research checks used Consensus original-record fetches, primary publisher/indexed records, official guidance, and Wiley passages. This is a targeted audit, not a new systematic review or independent replication of the underlying trials.

## Newly screened Wiley findings

Wiley Scholar Gateway · concurrent endurance/strength and plyometric programming in healthy adults/adolescent athletes · 5 passages · 3 articles · publication dates 3 November 2022–19 March 2026.

The returned studies do not directly test the app's novice prescriptions, fixed six-jump dose or numerical RIR rules. We checked the returned primary passages/abstracts rather than treating an AI summary as a finding.

- [Tundidor-Duque et al., 2026](https://doi.org/10.1111/sms.70265) randomized 41 moderately trained men to measured squat velocity-loss thresholds plus running or running alone for eight weeks. Greater resistance-training fatigue affected the mix of adaptations. This supports attention to fatigue, but **does not** permit converting 15%/40% bar-velocity loss into app RIR settings. Only the abstract and practical-application/perspective passages were returned; complete methods/figures were not qualified.
- [Li et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.1111/sms.14764) synthesized 36 youth concurrent-training studies. The diverse populations, modalities and network comparisons cannot specify an exact unsupervised youth app routine or universal session-order rule. Returned abstract, methods and discussion passages were reviewed.
- [Filipas et al., 2022 online / 2023 issue](https://doi.org/10.1111/sms.14257) examined plyometrics with endurance-intensity distributions in well-trained runners. The returned discussion describes 60 drop jumps in the weekly plyometric session, a materially different intervention from six small reset jumps. Do not import its performance benefit into the app's introductory landing block. Only returned abstract/discussion/metadata were qualified.

## Verification and remaining limits

`node scripts/check-evidence-duration.cjs` passed after the two evidence-copy changes. It covers duration/budget behavior, prescribed rest, per-leg accounting, timed sequences, evidence-map integrity and date arithmetic. `node scripts/review-program-science.cjs 2026-10-13` regenerated all 154 per-plan entries after the current prescription/copy changes.

An independent adverse-feedback probe covered all 103 prerequisite transitions in the five staged/NHS pathways: 21 increases required comfortable/no-symptom feedback, 80 unchanged repeats and two non-increasing transitions stayed available, and 515 missing, partial, absent-set, unfinished-set or insufficient-duration cases were blocked. No regression was found in those cases. The separate training-engine lifecycle test reports 154 plans, 4,899 workouts, 31,550 sets and 1,387 save/restores with synthetic completed input. Client synchronization and release builds belong to the final integrated release check.

Running completion still records total session minutes and subjective feedback, not actual adherence to each jogging interval. Walking through prescribed jogging bouts can therefore look complete in the current data model. Guidance tells users to repeat the stage in that circumstance, but no claim of objectively verified running readiness is warranted. A future interval-adherence confirmation should distinguish successful planned bouts from comfortable walking before advancing running demand.

This audit does not claim hundreds of newly read full papers, a trial of any exact app recipe, a systematic correction/retraction review, medical clearance, installed-device acceptance or guaranteed individual results. Existing ledger entries marked pending remain pending; this report does not silently upgrade their review depth. A transient Consensus search-rate limit affected one additional split/full-body query; other original-record fetches succeeded. Publisher errors/challenge pages were not bypassed.

For future quality improvements, prioritize individualized next-block volume decisions from actual completion/recovery, explicit race and sport-performance pathways with suitable prerequisites, and a reviewed exercise-specific biomechanical workload map. Do not substitute superficial variation, generic disclaimers or inflated set counts for those capabilities.
