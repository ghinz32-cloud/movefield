Movefield privacy notice

Effective 9 October 2026 · prototype website and phone starter

Movefield helps you plan and record training on your own device. The project is maintained by the ghinz32-cloud repository owner. Local use is the default. The authenticated hosted website can offer optional encrypted account sync and separately requested AI feedback when its backend is configured. The public GitHub Pages website and phone starter do not provide authenticated account transport. Parent or coach sharing and wearable connections are unavailable.

Information you choose to enter

Your profile can include a name, age, goals, experience, available time and equipment. You can save plans, events, completed sets, measurements, effort, symptoms and notes. Movefield uses these records locally to display your training and propose changes for your review. Use only the information you want stored on this device. Sample training is fictional.

Local use does not upload your training records. The optional account and feedback features below send only the data described for those features. Movefield has no advertising or analytics SDK, and does not sell training data. It does not read your contacts, location, microphone, camera, HealthKit or Health Connect.

Storage and security

The website stores encrypted training in this browser. Its key belongs to the same browser storage. The phone starter stores authenticated encrypted training with a device-bound key in the operating system's secure storage. Appearance, reminder and preview preferences are separate local settings and are not all encrypted.

Local encryption does not lock an already open app. Someone who can use your unlocked device or run code with the app's privileges may see your records. Protect the device with its screen lock. Browser data can be cleared or evicted. Phone key loss, reinstall or restoring an operating-system backup on a different device may make encrypted records unreadable.

New phone saves are encrypted. An older copy may remain if migration cleanup or deletion fails. The phone reports cleanup status and offers a retry for copies it can safely identify. The app does not promise secure erasure from operating-system backups, files you shared, flash-storage remnants or other applications.

Optional encrypted browser account sync

Sync requires signing in on the authenticated hosted website, choosing an account encryption password and reviewing which saved copy to keep. Selecting an account copy replaces the local copy; it is not an automatic merge. Your browser encrypts the profile, plans, notes and workout records before uploading them. The account password and derived encryption key are not sent to the backend. The unlocked key is held in this browser session's memory. The server stores encrypted records, your account identifier, record identifiers, revisions and mutation receipts needed to confirm uploads.

Keep the account encryption password and a separate backup. Movefield cannot recover that password or decrypt account records without it. Signing in alone does not restore the encryption key. Encryption does not hide the existence, size or timing of account records from the hosting service.

While sync is unlocked, changes are saved locally with an encrypted upload queue before the app tries the network. Interrupted uploads can retry; account conflicts require review. Locking sync stops this session's uploads and drops its unlocked key, but previously queued encrypted changes stay on this device until you unlock the same account or reset local storage. Local reset and browser site-data deletion do not delete records already saved to the account server.

Separately requested daily workout feedback

The separate daily feedback request requires an explicit request for a locally saved, completed workout. It is offered only to adult profiles reporting no pain or concerns, with no current training hold, and excludes fictional sample workouts. The request sends the workout and request identifiers, completion time, confirmation of consent and adult eligibility, a no-symptom confirmation, and completed set metrics: exercise identifier, set number, repetitions, load and, when recorded, repetitions in reserve, duration and distance. It does not send your name, age, free-text notes, profile, plan or full training history.

These feedback metrics are readable by the backend, unlike the encrypted account sync records. If a model provider is configured, the backend sends the completed set metrics to that provider over HTTPS with a fixed instruction for a short workout retrospective. The browser and phone do not call a remote model endpoint or receive its API key. Feedback is unavailable without valid server-side provider configuration and credentials. The configured provider's own privacy and retention practices apply; no particular provider is claimed to be active by this notice.

The backend saves the request, processing status and result in its daily_ai_feedback table. Feedback appears only after the server saves the text and never changes your plan automatically. Stopping the browser's status check does not delete or cancel a job already accepted by the backend. Requests are limited to twelve new jobs per account in a rolling 24 hours and four unfinished jobs. When a new request arrives, unfinished jobs older than 24 hours whose processing leases have expired are marked failed and their saved metric arrays are cleared. Successful processing also clears its saved metric array. Saved feedback, request metadata and failed-job data otherwise remain on the server; there is no automatic expiry of all feedback records or account-deletion control in this prototype.

Personalized lift and workout coaching

The optional browser coach uses recorded sets, up to three comparable prior workouts per exercise, a 28-day attendance summary and a minimized adult training profile. It excludes your name, sex, exact age, exercise notes, event names and raw equipment setup labels. The browser model receives this context locally after your training has been saved. Downloads and use of your training context require separate confirmation. Automatic local coaching is separately optional, and runs only for a newly logged lift or workout; editing a profile does not automatically request another review. The model chooses which calculated observations and engine-owned review options to prioritize. It does not write prescriptions or apply plan changes.

A validated coaching selection is encrypted in this browser before it is displayed. The newest twelve context results are retained. The full prompt and coaching context are not saved in this result store. Changed records, relevant history, profile or plan constraints invalidate the displayed selection. Unsupported hardware, interrupted work and invalid responses leave the normal calculated training review available; that review is labeled separately from AI.

Secure cloud coaching is a separate, explicitly approved request on the account-enabled hosted website. The minimized coaching context is readable by the backend and its configured model provider; it is not an opaque encrypted account-sync record. The server cannot read canonical plaintext workouts from encrypted account-sync records, so submitted context, adult eligibility and recorded symptom feedback are client assertions. Missing symptom feedback stays unknown and permits observations only, without progression choices. Provider credentials stay on the server. GitHub Pages and native clients have no configured account-backed cloud coaching connection. No provider credentials have been supplied for the current prototype.

Cloud coaching jobs are stored in workout_ai_coaching. Completed results appear only after the server saves a validated selection. Successful, failed and cancelled jobs clear the submitted context, while their receipt and terminal result metadata remain. Expired unfinished jobs are retired when another request is processed. Stopping cloud coaching makes a bounded cancellation attempt; disconnection can prevent cancellation from reaching the server. An explicit retry may resume a cancelled receipt within 24 hours with the same context/provider binding and remaining attempts; checking status never resumes it. V1 daily feedback and personalized coaching share twelve new jobs per account in 24 hours and four unfinished jobs. Each job permits at most three provider attempts. Provider retention and any free-plan limits follow the configured provider's policy. There is no automatic deletion of all saved server results or account-deletion screen.

Backups, imports and deletion

A password-protected transfer file contains your encrypted backup. Anyone with both the file and its password can read it; a lost password cannot be reset. A plain backup or recovery copy can contain readable personal information. Keep copies private and check that a transfer is saved before deleting your only records.

The phone's file picker reads only a file you choose. A share sheet sends the selected export to the application or destination you choose. That destination's privacy practices apply. Movefield tries to delete temporary import/export cache copies after use, but interruption or denied deletion can leave a copy until cleanup or operating-system reclamation.

Local records stay until you replace, delete or reset them, or the device/browser removes them. Export anything you want to keep first. The website's reset and your browser's site-data controls affect this browser; phone reset affects this installation. They do not delete server-held account records or AI feedback. The prototype has no account-deletion screen; contact the maintainer privately about server-held data. Separately delete exported files and backups wherever you saved them. Removing a plan is not the same as deleting its completed workout history.

Optional reminders and model files

Reminders and rest alerts use local operating-system notifications after you enable them. Notification permission is optional, and you can turn alerts off in Settings or your device's notification controls. Messages can appear on your lock screen according to your operating-system settings.

Optional Qwen model downloads start only after you review and confirm the specific files. The file host and its delivery providers receive normal download information such as your IP address and requested file. The app sends no workout record with that request. Model files are checked against pinned sizes and hashes and can be deleted in Settings; cache eviction may remove them. Native model inference is not connected. The optional browser Qwen3.5 coach uses your approved minimized training context on this device. The separate older Qwen sample uses fictional training. The authenticated host sends a restrictive background-worker security policy; the public Pages coach additionally requires its current hash-checked offline worker to supply the same restrictive policy before model execution. A first Pages visit may need a reload after offline-worker installation. No physical-device model accuracy, GPU memory, speed, heat or battery qualification is claimed.

Website hosting and external links

GitHub Pages delivers the public website files. GitHub and its delivery providers may process connection and request information, including your IP address, under the GitHub privacy statement. The authenticated hosted website also uses its hosting and database services to deliver the app and process the optional account features. Sign-in is handled by the hosting platform; the backend uses the authenticated account identifier and checks the dispatched identity's email, without storing that email in its account tables. Optional model files are hosted by Hugging Face and its delivery providers; see the Hugging Face privacy policy. Visiting an exercise or research source opens another website with its own privacy practices.

Offline caches contain the public app code and exercise/research content, not exported training records or downloaded model weights. This website shares a GitHub Pages origin with other projects from the same owner; the app's offline cache and worker are scoped to this project.

Youth and health information

The current prototype is for ages 14 and above. Youth training needs qualified supervision, and remote AI feedback is unavailable to youth profiles. No parent or coach automatically receives a young person's records. Movefield is not a medical device, does not diagnose symptoms or provide medical clearance, and its exact workout combinations have not been independently validated for individual results.

Contact and changes

Contact the project maintainer through the Movefield issue tracker. For a privacy question, post only a request to arrange a private contact channel. Do not post names, health information, passwords, recovery copies or training backups in public issues. Store distribution requires a verified support/privacy contact and final store disclosures; this notice does not claim store approval.

If the model provider, account retention, integrations or other data practices change, this notice and the relevant app disclosures must change before the new practice is enabled. The effective date identifies this revision.
