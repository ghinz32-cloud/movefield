Movefield privacy notice

Effective 9 October 2026 · local prototype website and phone starter

Movefield helps you plan and record training on your own device. The project is maintained by the ghinz32-cloud repository owner. This notice describes the current prototype. Real accounts, automatic cloud sync, parent or coach sharing, and wearable connections are not available.

Information you choose to enter

Your profile can include a name, age, goals, experience, available time and equipment. You can save plans, events, completed sets, measurements, effort, symptoms and notes. Movefield uses these records locally to display your training and propose changes for your review. Use only the information you want stored on this device. Sample training is fictional.

The app does not upload your training records to the developer, an advertising service or a remote AI service. It has no advertising or analytics SDK, and does not sell training data. It does not read your contacts, location, microphone, camera, HealthKit or Health Connect.

Storage and security

The website stores encrypted training in this browser. Its key belongs to the same browser storage. The phone starter stores authenticated encrypted training with a device-bound key in the operating system's secure storage. Appearance, reminder and preview preferences are separate local settings and are not all encrypted.

Local encryption does not lock an already open app. Someone who can use your unlocked device or run code with the app's privileges may see your records. Protect the device with its screen lock. Browser data can be cleared or evicted. Phone key loss, reinstall or restoring an operating-system backup on a different device may make encrypted records unreadable.

New phone saves are encrypted. An older copy may remain if migration cleanup or deletion fails. The phone reports cleanup status and offers a retry for copies it can safely identify. The app does not promise secure erasure from operating-system backups, files you shared, flash-storage remnants or other applications.

Backups, imports and deletion

A password-protected transfer file contains your encrypted backup. Anyone with both the file and its password can read it; a lost password cannot be reset. A plain backup or recovery copy can contain readable personal information. Keep copies private and check that a transfer is saved before deleting your only records.

The phone's file picker reads only a file you choose. A share sheet sends the selected export to the application or destination you choose. That destination's privacy practices apply. Movefield tries to delete temporary import/export cache copies after use, but interruption or denied deletion can leave a copy until cleanup or operating-system reclamation.

Records stay until you replace, delete or reset them, or the device/browser removes them. Export anything you want to keep first. The website's reset and your browser's site-data controls affect this browser; phone reset affects this installation. Separately delete exported files and backups wherever you saved them. Removing a plan is not the same as deleting its completed workout history. There is no developer account or server-held workout database to delete.

Optional reminders and model files

Reminders and rest alerts use local operating-system notifications after you enable them. Notification permission is optional, and you can turn alerts off in Settings or your device's notification controls. Messages can appear on your lock screen according to your operating-system settings.

Optional Qwen model downloads start only after you review and confirm the specific files. The file host and its delivery providers receive normal download information such as your IP address and requested file. The app sends no workout record with that request. Model files are checked against pinned sizes and hashes and can be deleted in Settings; cache eviction may remove them. Native model inference is not connected. The browser's experimental local sample uses fictional training, not your workout history.

Website hosting and external links

GitHub Pages delivers the public website files. GitHub and its delivery providers may process connection and request information, including your IP address, under the GitHub privacy statement. Optional model files are hosted by Hugging Face and its delivery providers; see the Hugging Face privacy policy. Visiting an exercise or research source opens another website with its own privacy practices.

Offline caches contain the public app code and exercise/research content, not exported training records or downloaded model weights. This website shares a GitHub Pages origin with other projects from the same owner; the app's offline cache and worker are scoped to this project.

Youth and health information

The current prototype is for ages14 and above. Youth training needs qualified supervision. No parent or coach automatically receives a young person's records. Movefield is not a medical device, does not diagnose symptoms or provide medical clearance, and its exact workout combinations have not been independently validated for individual results.

Contact and changes

Contact the project maintainer through the Movefield issue tracker. For a privacy question, post only a request to arrange a private contact channel. Do not post names, health information, passwords, recovery copies or training backups in public issues. Store distribution requires a verified support/privacy contact and final store disclosures; this notice does not claim store approval.

If accounts, remote processing, integrations or data practices change, this notice and the relevant app disclosures will change before those features are enabled. The effective date identifies this revision.
